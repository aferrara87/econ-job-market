About these numbers

Source
Listings on AEA JOE, counted by the date they went live. Ads on EconJobMarket that are not also on JOE are shown separately, because EconJobMarket keeps no history to compare with.
Entry level
Assistant professor, lecturer, postdoc, instructor, economist and similar posts, plus open-rank ads. Ads only for associate or full professors, chairs, directors or senior economists are left out, as are research assistant, predoc and internship ads.
Full-time and part-time
Full-time means permanent or tenure-track faculty. Part-time or temporary covers postdocs, visiting posts, teaching-track posts (lecturers and instructors in the US and Canada), fixed-term and adjunct posts.
Employer's rank
US jobs use the US News 2025 ranking of economics departments; jobs elsewhere use the QS 2026 ranking for economics and econometrics. A university's business, policy and other schools get the rank of its economics department. "Rest" includes unranked employers. Ranks apply to academic jobs only.
Typical year
Projection
Postings so far, divided by the share of the August–January total that had usually been posted by this date. The range uses the earliest and latest past seasons. It is a rule of thumb, not a forecast.
Fields
The JEL letters an employer chose for the listing. A listing can name several fields.
